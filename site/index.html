    BEHIND THE SCENE

    Reality, with
    a clear legend.

    The geography comes from APCRDA planning layers and OpenStreetMap. Landmark placement uses development parcels; landmark buildings are original illustrative massing, not official architectural models.

    Planning geometry
    APCRDA layers retrieved —: capital boundary, government parcels, — trunk-road centreline segments from — road layers, true-width road polygons, — blue-network features, bunds and — land allotments. Planning geometry is not proof of construction.
    Existing city
    — OpenStreetMap building footprints inside the capital boundary (OSM data as of —). Only — carry a floor count; the rest are drawn as an assumed single storey. — land-use polygons, the Krishna river, existing roads and rail come from the same source.
    Evidence styles
    Solid models mark work reported on site. Outlined models mark procured or planned work. Translucent masses are illustrative. A pulsing ring marks a project whose announced target falls due by the chosen date.
    Future views
    Evidence only shows nothing beyond published evidence. Announced targets marks projects due by the chosen date; a target is not delivery. Illustrative growth adds speculative massing. Compare keeps today's evidence on the left.
    Coverage and freshness
    Project evidence checked —. Datasets refresh through a reviewed pull request; project claims are curated by hand. No detailed BIM models or photographic textures.
    Rendering
    Locally bundled Three.js 0.184.0 with a physical sky and image-based lighting. Land-use colours, trees, water ripples and building tones are artistic. No satellite imagery is used. Quality adapts to the device; add ?quality=low to the address to force the lightest mode.
