BEHIND THE SCENE

Reality, with
a clear legend.

The geography comes from APCRDA planning layers and a capped OpenStreetMap footprint sample. Landmark placement uses development parcels; buildings are original illustrative massing, not official architectural models.

Mapped geometry
Government parcels, capital boundary, 72 road features and 102 water-network planning features.
Buildings
300 sampled OSM footprints have no height or floor-count tags. Their display height is a visual placeholder. Landmark dimensions and facades are illustrative.
Future views
6 months, 1 year, 2 years and the masterplan are scenarios. The controls vary visual treatments, not factual project status. Compare keeps today's geometry alongside the chosen scenario.
Coverage and freshness
Research checked 12 October 2026. Road geometry is partial: 19 of 35 sublayers downloaded. No automatic live refresh, detailed BIM models or current photographic textures.
Rendering
Locally bundled Three.js 0.184.0. Procedural landscape, trees and building details are artistic context. No satellite imagery is used.
